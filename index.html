Made Here

Sauces. Dough. Ricotta. Sausage. All of it.

Rocco's is a Belltown pizza joint and full bar at 2312 2nd Ave. We make everything from scratch here. Sauces, dressings, dough, ricotta, and sausage. We use local, organic, and artisan products whenever we can.

The bar is stocked with 120+ beers, a full cocktail program, and house-made bitters and tinctures we blend in-house - Sassafras, Lavender, Bucheron, and a few others. The space is brick, candlelit, and a little weird. That's on purpose.
